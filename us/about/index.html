About Lootura

A personal project to understand how the world moves, through the people who report on it.

I built Lootura. because I wanted to better understand what was happening around me. I read the news and moved from one publication to another, but struggled to follow the thread: what was changing, who was involved and how one story connected to the next. I wanted a place to explore the news with more context.

Lootura. brings news together around the people, places, companies and organisations that connect it. These entities help you discover why something is in the news, what happened before and how its story develops. The front page brings together stories with recent coverage, with links to their articles and publication dates. A clear ending lets you know you are all caught up.

The starting point is the press and its reporting. Headlines link to the original article and retain the publication and byline, when available. For me, seeking the truth starts with going to those sources, reading their reporting and comparing their accounts. Lootura. aims to make that journey easier and give visibility to the people reporting what happens.

Press is an experiment within Lootura. that focuses on the people behind the bylines. It brings together the articles we collect to explore what each author writes and which publications they write for. I want to see what we can understand about the news when we also follow the people reporting it.

The same curiosity is behind the website and the extension: opening a page or a new tab and finding a story that invites you to keep reading. This is a personal project, built to learn and find my bearings in the world. If it also helps you discover connections, ask questions and reach the sources, Lootura. has a purpose.

If you’d like to get in touch or share an idea, email me at hola@lootura.com.
