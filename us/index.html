    Jeff Bezos says he was ‘heavily involved’ in casting Robert Pattinson as James Bond Semafor · 20 LOOTS contributed
    Amazon founder Jeff Bezos said Tuesday he was “heavily involved” in the decision to cast Robert Pattinson as the next James Bond, the first under Amazon’s creative stewardship. “We ran a really tight process,” Bezos said at Semafor’s Silicon Valley & The World, adding that there were “real old-fashioned screen tests” for five finalists with “full costume, real sets.” He said on Friday the team spent a couple of hours reviewing the screen tests of the actors, saying that while all of them were “extraordinarily talented … [but] there was something special about Rob.” Bezos acknowledged that the selection committee was initially biased against Pattinson “because he’s already so famous,” and weighed going with someone who was “a little more unknown.” “But in the screening tests, it was so obvious he James Bond,” Bezos said. — Brendan Ruberry
