      SpaceX

      In the news for

      Musk makes $18 billion SpaceX stock pledge for every kid in America Axios · 8 LOOTS contributed
      Elon Musk on Wednesday pledged two SpaceX shares for every child in America, doubling an offer he made just last month. Why it matters: The $18 billion-plus giveaway roughly triples Michael Dell's once record-breaking Trump accounts gift . What they're saying: " Every child in America will receive two SpaceX shares, one from the company and one from me personally," he said in a post to X. On Oct. 19, he promised that every child would receive one share next year, adding it would be the "first of many." By the numbers: With about 72 million minors in the U.S. per Census estimates, and at SpaceX's closing price Wednesday, the gift would be worth around $18.35 billion. Musk owns billions of SpaceX shares, and the company has billions of shares outstanding, so the giveaway likely wouldn't have much impact on him or investors. Yes, but: The stock's fallen about 13% since he made the initial promise, amid a broader sell-off in AI-linked stocks. Zoom in: Musk's pledge isn't the first from SpaceX. In July the company's COO Gwynne Shotwell said she'd gift a share of the stock to each of 2 million Trump accounts. What's next: Musk did not offer a timing for the new distribution.
